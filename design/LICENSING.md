# Licensing

OxideBBS is licensed under the **Apache License, Version 2.0**.

## Why Apache-2.0?

Apache-2.0 is a permissive open-source license that works well for a contributor-friendly Rust project. It allows broad use, modification, distribution, and commercial use while also including an explicit patent grant.

This is a good fit for OxideBBS because:

- Sysops can freely run and customize their own boards.
- Contributors have a familiar, standard license.
- The project remains friendly to hobbyists, companies, and package ecosystems.
- The patent grant is clearer than MIT-only licensing.

## Contribution licensing

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, any contribution intentionally submitted for inclusion in OxideBBS is licensed under Apache-2.0, without additional terms or conditions.

## Door game warning

Do not bundle old DOS doors unless their license clearly allows redistribution.

A door being old, hard to buy, or commonly circulated does not automatically make it free to redistribute.

Project-created docs, examples, and ANSI assets in this repository are covered by Apache-2.0 unless a file explicitly says otherwise.
